Free help first

Try these before you call.

A surprising number of service calls end up being something a homeowner could fix in five minutes. We'd rather save you the trip charge. Run through these six checks first — they cost nothing.

Check 01

Thermostat

Set it to HEAT or COOL and push the temperature well past the room temperature so the system has a reason to kick on. Fresh batteries help too.

Check 02

Air filter

A clogged filter chokes the whole system. Pull it out and hold it up to a light — if you can't see through it, swap it for a new one.

Check 03

Vents & registers

Walk the house and make sure every vent is open and clear. Furniture, rugs, and curtains block more airflow than most people expect.

Check 04

Power switches

Your equipment has its own switches — one near the indoor unit and a disconnect box by the outdoor unit. Make sure both are ON.

Check 05

Breaker panel

Look for a tripped breaker at your electrical panel. Flip it fully OFF, then back ON. If it trips again right away, stop there and call us.

Check 06

Gas & pilot

For furnaces: confirm the gas valve on the line is open. On older units, check that the pilot light is lit and relight it per the door label.
